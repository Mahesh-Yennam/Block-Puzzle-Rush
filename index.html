💎 How to Play & Score Combos

Placement: Drag any block shape from the bottom tray onto the 10x10 board, or tap a piece and then tap a board cell to drop it.
Line Clears: Completely fill any horizontal row or vertical column across 10 squares to vaporize it and score bonus points.
Combos & Streaks: Clear multiple lines in one move or on consecutive turns to multiply your streak score up to 5x!
Survival: Keep board space open for larger pieces. The game ends when no pieces in your tray can fit anywhere.
